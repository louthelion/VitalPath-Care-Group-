Messaging Center Preparation

Messaging Center Preparation

One private place for messages is part of the plan — here is what it will do.

When the VitalPath platform opens, families and team members will message each other inside the platform instead of across scattered emails and texts: questions about your own information, updates, and coordination notes, kept together with the rest of your record.

The messaging center is in preparation and does not accept messages today. It will never be a channel for emergencies — urgent situations belong on the phone to 911 or to your care team. For general questions now, our contact page is the working door.

Kept together

Messages live beside the information they are about, so context is never lost between conversations.

Answered by people

Every message is read and answered by a member of the VitalPath team — with a record of who answered and when.

Private by design

Role-based access means a message is visible only to the people responsible for it.
