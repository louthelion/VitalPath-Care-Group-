Accessibility Statement

Accessibility Statement

VitalPath aims to support accessible, readable, responsive digital experiences.

VitalPath's work is about helping people — including people who read with assistive technology, people with low vision, and people who simply need clear words on a screen that works on any phone. Accessibility is not a feature we added at the end; it is part of how this site is built and maintained.

This statement describes the steps we take, the limits we honestly know about, and how to reach us if any page on this site is hard for you to use. A barrier you report is a bug we want to fix.

What we do

We build pages with plain language, readable type sizes, strong color contrast, headings that follow a logical order, and layouts that adapt to phones and tablets. We aim to follow recognized accessibility guidance (WCAG) as we build and revise.

Known limits

This site is young and still growing. Some older documents or third-party materials we reference may not meet the same standard yet. When we learn about a problem, correcting it goes on our work list — tell us and we will prioritize it.

Report a barrier

If any page is difficult to use, contact us at contact@vitalpathcaregroup.com or (407) 853-8199 and tell us which page and what went wrong. We will offer the information another way and work on the fix.
