Admin Dashboard Preparation

Future admin dashboard shell for intake review, resources, appointments, notices, and message routing.

This page describes a tool that is still being built. When VitalPath's platform opens, an internal admin dashboard will help our team review organized intake information, manage educational resources, coordinate appointments and notices, and route messages to the right person — in one place, with a clear record of what was done and when.

The dashboard is not active yet, and there is no sign-in on this page. Building it carefully — with privacy, role-based access, and clear records — matters more than opening it quickly. This page will be updated when the tool enters testing.

Intake review

Resources & notices

One place to keep learning materials current and to send the right notices to the right people at the right time.

Message routing

Questions and requests routed to the team member responsible, so nothing a family sends sits unanswered in the wrong inbox.
