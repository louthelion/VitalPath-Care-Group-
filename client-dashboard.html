Client Dashboard Preparation

Future client dashboard shell for requests, documents, appointments, notifications, and messaging.

When VitalPath opens, families will have a private dashboard of their own: a place to see their organized information, share documents securely, follow appointments and notifications, and message our team without repeating their story to a stranger every time.

That dashboard is in preparation and is not open for accounts today. The family pages of this website — the Learning Center, the Resource Library, and our contact page — are the working doors for now, and they stay free and open to everyone.
