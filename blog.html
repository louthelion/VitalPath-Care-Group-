Articles

Plain-language articles on care support, family caregiving, and getting organized — written by the VitalPath education team and added as each one is finished and reviewed.

Our articles answer one real question at a time: What does respite actually mean? What should be in a hospital-discharge folder? How do families split caregiving fairly? Each article is kept short enough to read in one sitting and careful enough to trust — information and education, never medical advice.

The library is growing week by week while VitalPath is in its preparation stage. Start with the starter article, browse the archive by topic, or visit the Learning Center for lessons organized like a course.
