Community Programs

Community Programs

Support groups, educational workshops, volunteer programs, partnerships, wellness events, family programs, resource fairs, and awareness events.

Community is not a decoration on VitalPath's work — it is half the work. Families caring for someone at home often feel alone with questions that a whole neighborhood has already answered. Our programs are being built to connect those people: to each other, to trustworthy information, and to the resources that already exist across Central Florida.

Programs open in stages during our preparation period, beginning with education and community connection. Anything that would involve direct care services waits for the registrations and approvals Florida requires — and we will always say plainly which stage each program is in.

Workshops & learning events

Plain-language sessions for families: understanding care options, preparing your home and paperwork, supporting a family caregiver, and planning for emergencies. Hosted with community partners around the Orlando area as the calendar opens.

Support & family programs

Peer support gatherings and family programs where caregivers can talk honestly, compare notes, and learn what worked for households like theirs. Caring for someone is easier when you are not the only one doing it.

Resource fairs & partnerships

We partner with churches, clinics, senior centers, and local groups to bring resource fairs and awareness events to the neighborhoods that need them — and to keep a current map of community help families can trust.
