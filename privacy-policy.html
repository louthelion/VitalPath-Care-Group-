Privacy Policy

Privacy practices for inquiries, forms, communications, and website use.

Who we are. This website is operated by VitalPath Care Group LLC, a healthcare learning center and community support platform in its preparation stage, based in Central Florida. This policy explains what information this website and our forms collect, and how we treat it.

What we collect. When you use a form on this site — an inquiry, an intake information form, or a worker interest form — we collect the information you choose to type into it, such as your name, contact details, and the notes you write. When you browse, our hosting tools may record standard technical information such as pages visited and device type.

How we use it. Information you send us is used to respond to you, to organize the information you asked us to organize, and to follow up when follow-up is needed. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use information from this website to make medical decisions, and this website is not a place to send urgent or emergency information.

Please be careful. Email and web forms are convenient, but they are not the safest place for detailed medical information. Share only what is needed for us to respond, and never send emergencies through this website — call 911 instead.

Sharing

We share information only when it is necessary to respond to your request, when a service provider helps operate this website (such as hosting), or when the law requires it. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

Your choices

You may ask us what information you have sent us, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to contact@vitalpathcaregroup.com and we will respond to reasonable requests.

Changes & questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions about privacy at VitalPath can be sent by email or raised by phone at (407) 853-8199.
