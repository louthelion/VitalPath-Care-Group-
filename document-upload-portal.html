Document Upload Portal Preparation

A secure place for documents is being built — here is how we will handle your papers, and what to do until it opens.

Families and professionals deal in important papers: identification, insurance cards, care plans, credentials. When VitalPath opens, a secure upload portal will let people share those documents with encryption and a clear record of what was received and when.

The portal is not open yet. Until it is, please do not email sensitive personal or medical documents to us — ordinary email is not a secure drawer. If you believe a document is needed for a question you have asked us, call first and we will agree on the safest way together.

Built for privacy

Encryption in transit, restricted access, and a receipt for everything uploaded — documents treated like the valuables they are.

What it will accept

Identification, insurance information, care-related paperwork, and professional credentials, in common file formats, with size guidance on the page.

Questions

Unsure whether something should be sent at all? Ask first: (407) 853-8199 or contact@vitalpathcaregroup.com.
