Services

Services

Family support intake, care professional interest, transportation coordination, community education, planning resources, and support coordination.

An honest note first: VitalPath Care Group is in its preparation stage, completing the registrations and approvals Florida requires. The areas below describe what is being built and what each one will do. Today, this page is information only — no care services are offered, scheduled, or sold through this website.

Family support intake organization is the center of the build. When the platform opens, families will complete one organized intake that gathers the facts a care conversation needs — who needs support, what daily life looks like, and what the family hopes for. Building that intake carefully, with privacy and dignity, is a central preparation task.

Community education

Workshops, articles, and learning sessions that explain care support in plain language: the types of help that exist, the questions to ask any provider, and how families can prepare. Education is available first, because informed families make better decisions.

Open the Learning Center

Planning resources

Checklists, worksheets, and guides that help a family get its information in order — emergency contacts, important documents, daily routines, and questions for providers — before a stressful moment forces quick decisions.

Browse the Resource Library

Transportation & care professional interest

Two preparation tracks run alongside the family side: information about transportation coordination, and a pathway for care professionals to register interest and learn screening expectations ahead of hiring. Both open formally when the platform launches.
