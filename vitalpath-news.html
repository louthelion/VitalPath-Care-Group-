News Center

Real health news for Central Florida families — dated stories, told in full on this page, with the source of every story named in plain text at its end.

The News Center collects healthcare and community news that matters to the families in our neighborhoods — public health updates, Florida healthcare changes, and community stories — summarized in plain language, with the original source always named. Stories run in two lanes: Florida & Community for the news closest to home, and National & World for the bigger picture.

A word on how to read health news, from us to you: check who published the original story, check its date, and be careful with headlines that promise miracles or spread fear. News can inform your questions; it should never replace your own doctor's advice about your own health. Summaries here are information only, never medical advice.
