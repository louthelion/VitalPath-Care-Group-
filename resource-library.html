Resource Library

Resource Library

Worksheets, checklists, and guides that help a family get its care information in order — being assembled and reviewed now, and published here in full as each one is finished.

Every resource in this library follows one rule: it must be useful at a kitchen table. Plain language, room to write, and nothing that requires a medical degree to understand. These are planning and organization tools — they do not diagnose, treat, or replace the advice of your own doctors and other professionals.

The first guides are in preparation while VitalPath completes its registrations. The descriptions below show exactly what each one will contain, so you can start organizing today with any notebook you have at home.

Family care folder checklist

The master list for the folder every family needs: doctors and pharmacies, insurance information, medication lists, emergency contacts, and important documents — with a place to note where each original is kept.

Emergency planning guide

A calm walk-through of preparing for storms, outages, and sudden hospital trips in Central Florida: what to pack, who to call, how to keep medicines and equipment safe, and how to reunite if you are separated.

Home safety & daily living guides

Room-by-room safety checks, a medication organization worksheet you keep yourself, transportation planning notes, and a family planning workbook for sharing care fairly among relatives.

Questions about a resource, or an idea for one your family needs? Contact us — reader requests shape this library.
