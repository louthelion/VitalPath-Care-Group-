Services

Services

Family support intake, care professional interest, transportation coordination, community education, planning resources, and support coordination.

This page is for organizations — senior living communities, clinics, churches, employers, and community groups — that work with families who may need care support information.

VitalPath Care Group is in its preparation stage, completing the registrations and approvals Florida requires. We are not accepting service requests or referrals at this time. What we can do today is share information: how the platform is being built, what responsible intake organization looks like, and how organizations can stay connected as opening approaches.

Information for organizations

Facilities and community organizations often see a family's need first. Our education materials are written to be shared: plain-language explainers and planning checklists your team can point families to while they make decisions.
