Careers

Explore future role pathways, worker interest, screening readiness, and professional development education.

VitalPath Care Group is preparing to build its team the right way: slowly, honestly, and with screening and training treated as sacred. We are not filling positions yet — hiring opens only when our registrations and approvals are complete. What is open now is interest: if you hope to work in care support, transportation support, coordination, or community education, you can register that interest today and learn what the work will require.

Every role at VitalPath will carry the same expectations: verified credentials where the role requires them, background screening, real training before the first assignment, and supervision that continues after it. If that sounds like the kind of team you want to join, we would rather meet you early than quickly.

Care professional pathway

For aides, companions, and other care professionals: register your interest, learn the screening and credential steps the field requires, and be first to hear when applications formally open.

Transportation pathway

For drivers and transportation support: safe driving, reliability, and respect for riders are the whole job. Register interest now; formal requirements and screening are explained on the form.

General worker interest

Not sure which pathway fits? Use the general worker interest form to tell us about yourself and the work you hope to do. Interest forms organize information for follow-up — they are not applications for an open job, and we will say so plainly whenever that changes.
