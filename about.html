About Us

About Us

VitalPath Care Group LLC is a healthcare learning center and community support platform being built in Central Florida — created to help families understand care support, get organized early, and walk into important decisions with clear information.

Most families meet the care system in a hard moment: a hospital discharge, a parent who suddenly needs help at home, a diagnosis that changes daily life. In that moment, the hardest part is often not love or effort — it is information. What options exist? What questions should we ask? What papers should we have ready? VitalPath exists to answer those questions calmly, in plain language, before the hard moment arrives.

VitalPath is being built in four parts: a learning center with plain-language education, a resource library of checklists and planning tools, a community connection point for programs and local resources, and an organized intake system that will help families present their information clearly when the platform opens. Education and community resources come first; everything else follows the proper approvals.

Where we are today

VitalPath is in its preparation stage. We are completing the registrations, licensing steps, and approvals that Florida requires before care-related services can be offered. Until that process is complete, this website provides education and information only — no care services are offered or scheduled here.

How it will work when it opens

When the platform opens, families will be able to organize their information once, learn what each support option really involves, and connect with the right next step in an orderly way — with screening, credentials, and contracts handled the responsible way, before any service begins.
