Worker Dashboard Preparation

Future worker dashboard shell for profile, availability, documents, scheduling, and messages.

Team members will one day have a dashboard of their own: profile and credentials in order, availability kept current, documents and training records together, and scheduling and messages in one place.

That tool is being built alongside our registrations and is not active yet. There is no worker sign-in on this page. If you hope to join the future team, the interest forms on our Careers page are the open door today.

Profile & credentials

A single home for the documents and credentials each role requires, with reminders before anything expires.

Availability & scheduling

Availability you control, matched carefully with assignments — when assignments exist, after our approvals are complete.

Training & messages

Training records, new lessons, and team messages in one place, so preparation is never a scavenger hunt.
